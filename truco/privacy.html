Privacy Policy

Truco: Noche en la pulpería

Play with peace of mind: this game does not collect, store on any server, or share any of your data.

Last updated: October 24, 2026 EspañolEnglish
  • No personal dataNothing is collected
  • OfflineOnly the purchase goes through Google Play
  • No adsNo trackers or analytics
  • Almost no permissionsVibration and Google Play billing

In short: no account or sign-up, no ads, and it plays offline. The only purchase is the full game (a one-time payment), processed by Google Play: the game never sees or stores your payment details. The only things saved are your settings and progress, and they are saved only on your device.

1Who we are

Truco: Noche en la pulpería ("the game") is developed by Hollowfire Studio. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

2What is saved on your device

To remember your preferences and progress, the game saves only on your own device:

  • Settings: music, effects and voice volume, rival speed, language, game hints, display options and vibration.
  • Your progress: how many matches you played and won against each rival, your records, the school, challenges and achievements, the in-game reales and what you bought with them, whether you own the full game, and any unfinished run.

This information never leaves your device: it is not sent to Hollowfire Studio or anyone else.

3How to delete your data

You can erase your results against rivals at any time from Settings → Game → Progress against rivals → Erase. To delete everything, uninstall the game or clear its data from your device settings.

4Permissions

The game requests only two permissions: vibration (in-match cues; it can be turned off in the settings) and Google Play billing (to buy the full game). It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

5Third-party services

The game includes no advertising, analytics or tracking services. The full game purchase is handled by Google Play (Google Play Billing), under Google's privacy policy: the game only receives confirmation that the purchase was made and saves it on your device. The store you downloaded it from (for example, Google Play) has its own privacy policy, which applies to the download and the store itself.

6Children

The game does not collect personal data from anyone, including minors.

7Changes to this policy

If the game ever adds features that change any of this (for example, online play), we will update this page before those features become available, with the date of the last update shown above.
